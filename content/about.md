Hi, I'm **Suzhongyu** (苏中玉). Online I usually go by **某不科学的虚**.

I work at the intersection of **mechanics** and **robotics**: how tendon-driven transmissions shape what a robot hand can do, how to model that faithfully, and how to make the simulated hand behave like the real one. My background is engineering mechanics, and I care about the gap between a clean equation and a robot that actually moves.

## Research interests

- **Tendon-driven robotic hands** — routing, friction, wrapping, transmission efficiency
- **Soft and continuum robotics** — constitutive models, static modeling, fabrication-aware design
- **Simulation and control** — MuJoCo models, tendon actuators, joint-space control loops
- **Embodied intelligence** — vision-language-action models, and what manipulation policies need from hardware
- **Mechanics-informed learning** — using structure instead of brute force

## What I'm working on

- Building and validating a **tendon-driven biomimetic hand in MuJoCo**, including tendon routing and an angle outer-loop controller.
- Re-implementing papers on dexterous manipulation with tendon-driven hands.
- Exploring **VLA models** for manipulation and thinking about the sim-to-real gap.
